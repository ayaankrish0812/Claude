Get Involved

Your old gear is
someone's first game

Three ways in. Pick the one that fits — gear, time, or partnership — and we'll handle the rest.

Donate Gear

Cleats, balls, gloves, helmets — if a kid can play with it, we can place it. Tell us what you have and we'll arrange drop-off or pickup within the Chicago area, or shipping from anywhere.

Start A Donation

Volunteer

Sort and refurbish gear at our Chicago warehouse, coach a season at a Community Court, or mentor an Athlete Pathways student. A few hours a month changes a season.

Join The Team

Partner

Schools, leagues, brands, and community centers: co-host a gear drive, sponsor a court build, or become a distribution partner in your region.
