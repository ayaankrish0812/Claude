Our Story

Started by high schoolers who live for sports

Sports Beyond Borders was founded in May 2024 in San Ramon, California, by high schoolers with a lifelong background in sports. We know what it feels like to step onto a field with the right gear — and we believe the lack of equipment should never be the reason a child misses out on sport.

So we run gear drives to collect cleats, shoes, and sports apparel from our community, team up with partners like Shoes for Senegal, and get it to kids who need it — from our own backyard to Senegal, Paraguay, and Nicaragua.
