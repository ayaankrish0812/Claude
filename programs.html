01

Gear Drive Core Program

The Gear Drive is the heart of Sports Beyond Borders. We collect new and gently used cleats, shoes, and sports apparel from families, teams, and neighbors in our community, then send it where it's needed most.

So far we've donated more than $12,000 in equipment to kids in Senegal, Paraguay, and Nicaragua. Our cleat collections add up fast — the pairs in this photo alone number over 40.

  • Cleats, shoes & sports apparel
  • Community collection drives & giveback events
  • Deliveries to kids in Senegal, Paraguay & Nicaragua
  • Drop-off and pickup around San Ramon

02

Free Day Camps

Our Free Day Camps give kids a place to play no matter what their family can afford. They're built around fun, teamwork, and learning the game — and they never cost families a dime.

Because we're athletes ourselves, we know the best part of any sport is simply getting to play with friends. The camps are designed to make that easy for every kid who shows up.

  • 100% free for every family
  • Games, drills & teamwork
  • Led by athletes who love the game

03

Partnered Sports Refurbishing

Not every piece of gear arrives game-ready. Through partnerships — including with Shoes for Senegal — we clean, repair, and refurbish used sports gear so it can be played in again.

It keeps good equipment out of the landfill, stretches every donation further, and gets more kids into gear that fits and works.

  • Partnerships with organizations like Shoes for Senegal
  • Cleaning, repair & refurbishing of used gear
  • Less waste, more game days
