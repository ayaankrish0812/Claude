Community Giveback

The Livermore giveback

Impact doesn't only happen overseas. In Livermore, California, we held a clothes giveback for underrepresented communities — tables of clothing, and kids and families picking out what they needed.

It's the same idea behind everything we do: when someone has more than they need and someone else has less, a few high schoolers with a plan can bridge the gap.

The Journey

From one idea in 2024
to kids around the world

April 2024

Sports Beyond Borders begins

A group of high school athletes in San Ramon, California starts collecting sports gear for kids who can't afford it.

Gear Drive

The cleats start piling up

Neighbors, teammates, and families donate cleats, shoes, and apparel — including a collection of more than 40 pairs of cleats.

Partnership

Teaming up with Shoes for Senegal

A partnership with Shoes for Senegal helps us refurbish used gear and get it to the kids who need it.

Giveback

Livermore clothes giveback

We bring a giveback to underrepresented communities in Livermore, California.

Going global

Gear reaches Senegal, Paraguay & Nicaragua

More than $12,000 in equipment reaches kids in about five countries.

Now

Free day camps & still going

We're running free day camps, growing our partnerships, and collecting more gear every week.

Our Belief
Access to play is a right, not a privilege.

It's the belief Sports Beyond Borders was founded on in April 2024 — and the reason we keep showing up, one pair of cleats at a time.
