01

Gear Drive Core Program

The Gear Drive is the heart of Sports Beyond Borders. We collect new and gently used cleats, shoes, and sports apparel from families, teams, and neighbors in our community, then send it where it's needed most.

Since our first drive in November 2024, we've run three gear drives and donated more than $12,000 in equipment to kids in Senegal, Paraguay, and Peru. Our cleat collections add up fast — the pairs in this photo alone number over 40.

  • Cleats, shoes & sports apparel
  • Community collection drives & giveback events
  • Deliveries to kids in Senegal, Paraguay & Peru
  • Drop-off and pickup around San Ramon

02

Free Day Camps Coming Soon

We're planning free day camps that give kids a place to play no matter what their family can afford. Each camp is built around three to four sports stations, with kids spending an hour at each one learning and playing the sport.

All the equipment is provided, and the water and snacks are on us. The goal is simple: make it easy for any kid to try new sports, get moving, and have fun with friends.

  • 3–4 sports stations
  • One hour at each station
  • All equipment provided
  • Free water & snacks
  • $0 for families

03

Partnered Sports Refurbishing In Planning

A lot of sports gear gets thrown away long before it's worn out. In November 2026 we started planning a refurbishing project that recycles tennis balls, rackets, and other sports equipment into new “used” equipment that can be donated to kids.

Partnerships are at the heart of the idea. We teamed up with Shoes for Senegal in June 2026, and we'd love more partners to help bring this project to life.

  • Recycling tennis balls, rackets & other sports equipment
  • Turning used gear into donation-ready equipment
  • Keeping playable gear out of the landfill
  • Built on partnerships — we'd love to team up
