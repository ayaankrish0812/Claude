Our Story

Breaking down barriers, one game at a time

Sports Beyond Borders was founded on a simple belief: the lack of equipment should never be the reason a child misses out on sport. We collect, refurbish, and distribute sporting gear to communities that need it most — because access to play is a right, not a privilege.

We don't just hand over equipment. We partner with local coaches, schools, and community centers to build lasting sports cultures that foster teamwork, resilience, and belonging.

How We Play

Three rules we never break

Community First

We work alongside communities, not for them. Local coaches and organizers lead; we supply and support.

Sustainable Impact

Refurbished gear reduces waste and stretches every dollar. Nothing playable ever hits a landfill.

Global Reach, Local Roots

International scope, always shaped by local needs. Eight countries, one playbook: listen first.
