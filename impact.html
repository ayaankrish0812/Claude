The Journey

From one duffel bag
to eight countries

2015

The first drive

Founded in Chicago with a single carload of donated soccer gear delivered to a South Side youth league.

2017

First Community Court

A cracked parking lot in Detroit becomes a painted, lit, full-court neighborhood hub — the model for 30 more.

2019

Going international

Partnerships in Kenya and Guatemala take the Gear Drive beyond U.S. borders for the first time.

2022

Athlete Pathways launches

Mentorship and scholarship program opens with 20 athletes; 26 scholarships awarded since.

2026

12,000 kids and counting

Eight countries, 47 communities, 18 sports — and a waiting list we're determined to clear.
