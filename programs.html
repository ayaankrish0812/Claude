What We Do

Three programs,
one goal

Collecting gear, building courts, and growing athletes. Every program starts with the same question: what's keeping kids off the field?

01

Gear Drive

We run year-round equipment drives, collecting gently used sporting goods from donors across the country. Every item is inspected, cleaned, and matched to a community in need — nothing playable ever goes to waste.

  • Soccer balls, cleats & jerseys
  • Basketball hoops & shoes
  • Baseball gloves & helmets
  • Equipment for 15 more sports
38K+Items Collected
100%Inspected & Cleaned

02

Community Courts ★ Core Program

We partner with local organizations to establish dedicated play spaces in underserved neighborhoods — from painted asphalt courts to full multi-sport fields, each with a plan to keep it alive for years.

  • Infrastructure support
  • Ongoing equipment supply
  • Certified coach placement
  • Youth league coordination
31Courts Built
47Communities

03

Athlete Pathways

For young athletes showing exceptional potential, we provide mentorship, scholarship support, and pathways to regional and national competitions — so talent gets a route, not a roadblock.

  • 1-on-1 mentorship
  • Academic scholarships
  • Competition sponsorships
  • Alumni network access
