The Livermore giveback

Impact doesn't only happen overseas. In Livermore, California, we held a clothes giveback for underrepresented communities — tables of clothing, and kids and families picking out what they needed.

It's the same idea behind everything we do: when someone has more than they need and someone else has less, a few high schoolers with a plan can bridge the gap.

The Journey

From one idea in 2024
to kids around the world

May 2024

Sports Beyond Borders is founded

High school athletes in San Ramon, California start Sports Beyond Borders to get sports gear to kids who can't afford it.

Sept 2024

Our club launches at California High

We bring Sports Beyond Borders to campus as a club at California High School.

Nov 2024

Our first gear drive

Neighbors, teammates, and families donate our first round of cleats, shoes, and apparel.

May 2025

First outreach program: Paraguay

Our first international delivery sends gear to kids in Paraguay.

July 2025

Second gear drive

Another round of donations comes in.

Dec 2025

Third gear drive

Our third drive brings in more cleats, shoes, and apparel.

March 2026

Livermore giveback

We hold a clothes giveback for underrepresented communities in Livermore, California.

June 2026

Teaming up with Shoes for Senegal

We partner with Shoes for Senegal to get shoes to kids who need them.

Nov 2026

Planning our refurbishing project

We start planning a program to recycle tennis balls, rackets, and other sports equipment into donation-ready gear.

Our Belief
Access to play is a right, not a privilege.

It's the belief Sports Beyond Borders was founded on in May 2024 — and the reason we keep showing up, one pair of cleats at a time.
